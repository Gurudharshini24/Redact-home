What is Redact Agent?

Redact Agent is a privacy-focused intelligent system designed to identify sensitive information from visual and digital content and automatically redact it before the information is exposed to external AI systems.

Core Capabilities

Detect

Automatically identifies sensitive information such as names, emails, phone numbers and passwords.

✂️

Redact

Masks sensitive information when the scanner reaches the detected content.

🔐

Reduces unnecessary exposure of private information while enabling AI-based processing.
